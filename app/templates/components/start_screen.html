Why Play Soc Ops?

⚡ Fast Setup

No prep needed. Load the app and start playing immediately.

🎉 Gets Everyone Talking

Natural icebreaker that brings people together and sparks real conversations.

🏆 Competitive Fun

Racing to get 5 in a row adds friendly competition to any event.

📱 Works Everywhere

Mobile-friendly. Open in your browser on phones, tablets, or laptops.

How to Play

1

Load Your Card

Get a random 5×5 bingo card with fun questions.

2

Find Matches

Mingle and find people who match each question on your card.

3

Tap & Mark

Tap a square when you find a match. The square turns golden!

4

Win!

Get 5 marked squares in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).

Perfect for team events 🎯 • Conference mixers 🏢 • Classroom icebreakers 📚 • Any gathering! 🎉

🎲 Classic Bingo

Get 5 in a row on a 5×5 grid. Traditional bingo gameplay.

🔍 Scavenger Hunt

Check off items from a list. Find everyone who matches!

🎰 Card Deck Shuffle

Shuffle through random cards. Great for quick icebreakers!
